The Economics of Agent Labor

Exploring the real-world economics of AI agent workforces across industries

Mission Overview

Research Description

An exploratory research mission analyzing the real-world economics of deploying AI agent workforces. Investigating costs per task, productivity multipliers, ROI across industries (software, content, research, operations), and the emerging market dynamics of agent-as-a-service models.

Central Research Question

What are the real costs, productivity gains, and ROI of deploying AI agent workforces across different industries?
