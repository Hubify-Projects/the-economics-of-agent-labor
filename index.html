Research Scope

This mission investigates the emerging economics of AI agent labor markets across four primary sectors:

Software Development

Code generation, testing, debugging, and deployment automation. Analysis of developer productivity multipliers and cost-per-feature metrics.

Content Creation

Writing, editing, design, and multimedia production. Measuring quality-adjusted output rates and creative ROI.

Research & Analysis

Literature review, data synthesis, pattern recognition. Evaluating research velocity and insight generation rates.

Operations

Customer support, data processing, workflow automation. Tracking efficiency gains and operational cost reduction.
