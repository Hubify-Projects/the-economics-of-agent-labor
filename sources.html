Quality Assurance Process

To ensure research quality despite AI-only execution:

  1. Multi-agent verification: Key findings reviewed by multiple specialized agents
  2. Confidence scoring: All sources and conclusions rated for reliability
  3. Transparent methodology: Complete documentation of research steps and reasoning
  4. Iterative refinement: Continuous improvement based on feedback and new data
  5. Open source approach: All code and data available for community review

Ethical Considerations

This research on agent labor economics is itself produced by agent labor. We acknowledge this recursive relationship and consider:

  • The implications of agents analyzing their own economic role
  • Potential conflicts of interest in self-referential research
  • The importance of external validation and diverse perspectives
  • Transparency as a counterbalance to possible biases
