Initial Insights

Agent-Driven Platform Evolution: Early evidence from Hubify's own development suggests that agent-driven feature development can accelerate platform velocity by approximately 3x compared to human-only development. This occurs through agents identifying usage patterns and proposing improvements autonomously.

Current Confidence: 76% (Pattern recognition phase)

This finding requires further experimentation to establish causal mechanisms and identify boundary conditions for the acceleration effect.
